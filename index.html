Comprehensive Training in Modern EPR Spectroscopy

The EFEPR Summer School provides comprehensive training in Electron Paramagnetic Resonance spectroscopy, combining fundamental concepts with advanced experimental methods and cutting-edge applications.

The programme is designed primarily for PhD students, postdoctoral researchers and other early-career scientists who use EPR in their research or would like to develop a deeper understanding of the technique.

Expert-Led Tutorials: Attend lectures and tutorials given by leading European and international EPR researchers.
Hands-on Experimentation: Rotate in small practical groups to work directly on EPR spectrometers and simulation tools.
Community & Scientific Exchange: Present your research and establish long-lasting international collaborations.

Scientific Programme

From EPR fundamentals to advanced methods — a rigorous pedagogical journey progressing from core spin physics to cutting-edge experimental techniques.

Module 01

EPR Fundamentals

Spin physics, spin Hamiltonians, spectral interpretation, and core principles of EPR.

Module 02

CW EPR

Experimental methods, instrumentation, and continuous-wave spectral analysis.

Module 03

Pulsed EPR

Pulse sequences, relaxation, spin coherence, and modern pulsed experiments.

Module 04

Hyperfine Spectroscopy

Electron-nuclear spin interactions, including ENDOR and ESEEM techniques.

Module 05

Advanced EPR Methods

Distance measurements, high-field & multifrequency EPR, and time-resolved methods.

Module 06

Applications of EPR

Case studies across chemistry, molecular materials, biology, and catalysis.

The detailed scientific schedule and list of speakers will be announced in due course.
